4. Zero-Telemetry Privacy & Grid Discretization Sequence

To fully eliminate the risk of reverse-engineering geographic paths or executing linkage attacks, the architecture utilizes a Discrete Global Grid System (DGGS) protocol to enforce zero-telemetry guarantees:

Edge Spatial Interception: Upon capturing raw spatial telemetry (Latitude and Longitude tokens), the edge framework immediately polyfills and converts coordinates into an alphanumeric hexagonal spatial index utilizing the 'Uber H3' format.

GPS Purging & Relational Joins: To eliminate long-term spatial tracking, raw coordinates and granular timestamps are immediately wiped from volatile memory and transaction logs, preventing any disk serialization. Atmospheric and environmental datasets are joined to maternal profiles exclusively via this H3_index relational key.

Dynamic k-Anonymity Geometry Shift: The pipeline analyzes local population metrics to dynamically enforce k-anonymity safety thresholds. Urban zones with high population density maintain fine meso-resolution cells. Conversely, sparse or rural areas where data density drops below k < 100 trigger an automated parent-resolution function, permanently and irreversibly blurring the geometric grid resolution to a macro-zone boundary before committing data to the database.

Metadata Sanitation: All clinical facility identifiers, real names, and personal tokens are entirely cleansed, ensuring total compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and PHI standards, rendering the sample safe for public distribution.
